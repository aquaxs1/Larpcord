The fine print, in large type

Larpcord vs. regular Discord

Larpcord is purely cosmetic and local. Everything Discord's servers check stays exactly as it is.

TopicRegular DiscordLarpcord
AppOfficial clientIts own .exe, open source (GPL-3.0), based on Vesktop & Vencord
Badges & Nitro lookOnly what you really haveAnything you want – only you see it
NameChanges have a cooldown and are visible to everyoneInstant, no cooldown – local only; others see your real name
Server orderSynced across devicesYour own layout only in Larpcord; regular Discord and your phone stay as before
ActivityYour real activityYour own activity locally – the gateway still gets the real one
Roles & serversReal roles and server namesLarp roles without permissions; locally renamed servers keep their name for everyone else
Uploads & streamingBased on your real subscriptionUnchanged – no bigger uploads, no real HD streaming, no real boosts
Your account–Never changed: no write requests to Discord for larp values
Layout rescue–Hold Shift on startup to reset the layout
Important: Client mods violate Discord's Terms of Service. Use at your own risk. And please: don't trick other people with fake badges – Larpcord is for your own fun.

⬇ Download Larpcord (.zip)

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · The ZIP contains Larpcord-Setup.exe

  1. Extract the ZIP and run Larpcord-Setup.exe – one click, no admin rights.
  2. SmartScreen? Larpcord is not code-signed: “More info” → “Run anyway”.
  3. Log in and get started under Settings → Larpcord → Larpcord Hub.
