Differences

Larpcord vs. Discord

Discord Larpcord
Badges & Nitro What you paid for Anything – only you see it
Name Cooldown Instant, local only
Server order Synced Your own, only in Larpcord
Activity & roles Real Anything, local only
Uploads & streaming Your plan Unchanged
Your account – Never touched

Client mods break Discord's Terms of Service – use at your own risk. Don't fool people with fake badges.

Download Larpcord

Windows 10/11 · Android · iOS

Download (.exe) Android (.apk) iOS (.ipa) GitHub

Windows

  1. Run Larpcord-Setup.exe
  2. SmartScreen: “More info” → “Run anyway”
  3. Log in → Settings → Larpcord

Android

  1. Open Larpcord.apk on your phone
  2. Allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser when asked
  3. Log in → Settings → Larpcord

iOS

  1. Install Larpcord.ipa with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly (your Apple ID signs it)
  2. Free Apple IDs: refresh the app every 7 days in the sideloading tool
  3. Log in → Settings → Larpcord

On phones, Larpcord is Discord's web app with the Larpcord plugins, shown at tablet width. Voice and video depend on the web version.
